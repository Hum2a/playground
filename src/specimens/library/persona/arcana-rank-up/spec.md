# Arcana Rank-Up (portable recreation spec)

Social-link bond card rises from rank 3 to 4 with RANK UP flourish stamp.

## Sequence

1. Rise — card lifts and wobbles (520ms)
2. Flourish — RANK UP stamp appears (360ms)
3. Done — rank 4 displayed

## Reduced motion

Rank 4 and flourish shown immediately.

## Palette
