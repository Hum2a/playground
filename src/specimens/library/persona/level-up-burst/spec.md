# Level-Up Burst

Level rolls upward once; stats reorder under a gold burst.

## Sequence

1. Roll — LV 12→13 (120ms cadence)
2. Stats — HP / SP / STR stagger in (120ms gaps, 280ms transition)
3. Done — finals hold until reset

## Reduced motion

Finals appear immediately.
