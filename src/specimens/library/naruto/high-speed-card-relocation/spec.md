# High-Speed Card Relocation

A card vanishes mid-travel while two afterimages mark the path, then docks in slot B.

## Behaviour

- Idle: card in slot A.
- Travelling: live card hidden; two ghosts at 0% / 25%.
- Arrived: card visible in slot B; slot A empty.
- Reduced motion: card in slot B immediately.

## Freeze

- Travel: 200ms · ghosts: 2 @ life [220, 150]ms · spawn [0, 60]ms · settle: 40ms
- Palette (wind): `#0C1014` / `#D0D6DC` / `#8AA0B4` / `#5A6870` / `#EEF2F4`
