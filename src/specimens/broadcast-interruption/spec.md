# Broadcast Interruption

Persona 4–adjacent urgent transmission: normal programming is forcibly replaced by an override card with yellow flash punctuation.

## Visual intent

- Stable bulletin card in cream (`#F7F3E8`) on ink (`#101010`)
- Yellow flash (`#F5C400`) punctuates the cut
- Urgent card slams in with inverted tag treatment
- Palette: `#F5C400`, `#101010`, `#F7F3E8`, `#E8E8E8`

## Interaction

- Trigger button runs interrupt sequence (~620ms)
- Reduced motion: instant swap to urgent card

## Reduced motion

Disable flash and slide animations under both RM triggers; urgent card appears immediately.

## Accessibility

- Live region announces programming vs urgent state
- Trigger button has `:focus-visible` ring
